TartaTV is an Android TV client that plays IPTV lists in the Xtream Codes
standard. You provide your own server URL, username and password — the app
does not distribute, host or sell any content. Playback depends entirely on
the list you supply.

Features:

* Live channels grouped by category, with XMLTV EPG (current programme and
  upcoming days)
* Movies (VOD) with poster, plot, cast, director, genre, rating, duration,
  and resume from where you stopped
* Series with seasons, episodes, autoplay of the next episode, watched marks
  and per-episode progress
* "Continue watching" home, consolidating movies and episodes in progress
* Recommendations based on what you watched (computed locally, no telemetry)
* Watchlist separate from Favorites
* Global search by name, genre, cast, plot — over a local FTS4 index
* Advanced filters (year, rating, genres) per movies/series category
* Parental PIN (encrypted) with mandatory setup before first adult-category
  access
* Multi-profile (separate Xtream accounts, separate favorites/progress) and
  Kids profile with allow-listed categories
* Picture-in-Picture and a persistent mini-player when you leave full screen
* Time-shift on channels that support tv_archive (rewind 30 min / 1 h / 2 h)
* Offline catalog cache (TTL 6h, refreshed in background) and offline detail
  cache (TTL 24h)
* Auto-update via GitHub Releases with full changelog in-app
* Notifications: new episodes on favourite series, catalog refresh complete,
  resume reminder, server errors
* Languages: Portuguese, English, Spanish, Italian, French, German

Privacy:

* No telemetry, analytics or crash reporting — everything stays on your
  device
* Credentials and PIN are stored encrypted (AES-256, EncryptedSharedPreferences)
* No Android cloud backup of credentials or cache
* Only requests INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE
  permissions

The TartaTV name and mark belong to their holder.
